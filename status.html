Where it stands

What works, what is being worked on, and what is open: in the nesting itself and in the example setups.

Nesting

The method and the code

Working

  • 2D two-way nesting over any number of levels; volume conserved to round-off.
  • Active regions, land-aware exchanges, M:N exchange plans between any tilings; bit-identical across process splits.
  • One MPMD component per model; load balancing with nesting-balance, launch by mpiexec or Slurm.
  • The sub-stepping instability understood and removed by an AGRIF-like sponge.
  • 3D: temperature and salinity between levels with different vertical grids: rim, ring, 3D transports, ring velocities and the 3D flux register; heat conserved to 1e-9.

In progress

  • IDEAL V2, a stratified lake at rest, against each level's grid run alone: after a fix of the remap's outer layers the coupling adds nothing in the channel, 1.4× on the shelf and 2.1× in the strait (one face where a wall meets the interface; being looked at).
  • IDEAL V5, a pycnocline over the shelf slope against one level at 333 m: within 8–13 cm of pycnocline depth in every level, mostly from the coarser levels' layers.
  • IDEAL V6, a dye front through the levels: conserved to 4e-9; new extrema 1e-5 in the children after limiting the ring correction, 2e-4 in the parent from the flux correction.
  • IDEAL V7, the cost: with the compiled remap kernel (15× faster than numpy) the 3D exchange is 4–9 % of a run with T and S, 8–17 % with 14 tracers, 3–8 % with 32 tracers and a FABM-like load; with that load the balanced split is 5–9 % faster than the uniform one.
  • The fork follows pyGETM v0.9.29 (merged 3 November 2026).
  • Drying and flooding: layouts keep the interfaces out of water that floods and dries (interface_min_depth, checked at start-up); drying happens inside a level. In 2D the coupling also handles a flat across an interface (a safety net). In 3D pyGETM itself needs a small bottom roughness on flats (z0 ≈ 0.001 m).
  • Restart of a nested run: pyGETM's 2D restart is now exact, the nesting's state is in the restart files; not yet bit-identical.

Open

  • nesting-balance with every cost calibrated (2D and 3D exchanges): IDEAL on 12 cores 7 % faster balanced; predictions within 5–13 %.
  • The safeguard against new extrema from the flux correction on the parent's side (2e-4 in IDEAL V6).
  • Limited slopes and time interpolation in the 3D ring.
  • Interfaces between sibling children that touch (when a setup needs them).
  • Adaptive vertical coordinates (last; GVC and SIGMA until then).
  • Running a single model with its boundaries from a file.
  • One process split in IDEAL E8 (1e-4 m).
Examples

The setups

IDEAL

  • 2D: E1, E2, E3, E8, E9 done.
  • 3D with temperature, 10 / 15 / 20 layers: conservation and uniformity passed; the lake at rest (V2), a pycnocline over the slope (V5), a dye front (V6) and the cost (V7) done.

NEAB

  • Five levels, 31-day tidal runs on 64 cores (coarse); gauge and TPXO9 comparisons.
  • The tide east of the Skagerrak amphidrome is open: the depth datum (LAT → MSL) and the Wadden Sea flats are the main levers so far.
  • Next: NEAB in 3D, after IDEAL V2–V7.

DKm

  • Ten levels down to 57 m from Denmark's Depth Model; open boundaries from NEAB, TPXO9 or CMEMS.
  • A nested variant (the Belt Sea inside the Kattegat) gives the same tide on fewer cores.
  • Automatic thalwegs from the 50 m source for every level (nesting-thalwegs), with a page per level, applied in the bathymetry: Roskilde Fjord and the Bodden are joined to the sea. DKm's bathymetry now comes from ocean-prep (DDM 50 m, closed passages, only the main water); its first tidal run (TPXO9, 1 + 7 days) matches v4 at the gauges and is closer in the Belts (tides); Thyborøn is lower.
  • Open: water cut off from the sea in the fine levels, the dredged channels, sluices, the island fjords, the Wadden Sea.

Two-way, multi-level nesting for pyGETM, developed by Bolding & Bruggeman. Code: the fork bolding/pygetm (branch nesting-mpmd); the setups and tools (pygetm-nesting) are not public yet.
