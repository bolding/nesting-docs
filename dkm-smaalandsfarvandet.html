Setup

The level in dkm.yaml

smaalandsfarvandet:
  parent: belt_sea
  istart:  162
  istop:  282
  jstart:  102
  jstop:   210
  ratio:   3
  time_ratio: 3
  min_depth: 0.5          # shallow sounds: drying and flooding
  source: ddm
  active:
    seed: [11.4, 55.05]     # Smålandsfarvandet
    barriers: []

# Ringkøbing Fjord (like Nissum Fjord) reaches the North Sea only through a
# sluice (Hvide Sande): its active region does not touch the parent's water,
# so it has no interface. It needs a sluice connection (a culvert or a
# source/sink pair between child and parent), not ordinary nesting.

The layout's config block sets the source (DDM completed with EMODnet) and the default min_depth 0.5 m (this level its own).

Bathymetry

What the grid keeps of the source

  • The level's own water (not under its children, not behind its barriers): 3,447 km² on the grid, 3,415 km² deeper than min_depth in the source.
  • Source water in the level's dry cells: 20.2 km² (0.6 %), 16.7 million m³. In its wet cells 1.5 % of the source cells are land or shallower.
  • Inside a wet cell the source depths spread by 0.7 m (p90 − p10, median over the cells; 2.4 m in the 10 % most varied). The block mean lies at the 50th percentile of its cell's source depths (10–90 % of the cells: 40th–60th).
  • 6 basins of at least 4 cells with no connection found in the source (deeper than min_depth): 116 cells at 11.69°E 55.20°N; 76 cells at 12.29°E 54.98°N; 25 cells at 11.74°E 55.13°N; 17 cells at 11.47°E 55.20°N; 15 cells at 11.28°E 55.25°N; 9 cells at 11.01°E 54.81°N.

Thalwegs

Source water deeper than 4 m (then down to min_depth for basins still cut off) that crosses the level's dry cells and joins two basins (gap) or two parts of one basin much further apart around it (shortcut). Through each, the path whose shallowest point is deepest; each dry cell on it gets the median of the source depths along the path in that cell, not its deepest point.
