Setup

The level in dkm.yaml

# ── Ringkøbing Fjord: 8.0..8.467E, 55.78..56.14N ────────────────────────────

The layout's config block sets the source (DDM completed with EMODnet) and the default min_depth 0.5 m (this level its own).

Bathymetry

What the grid keeps of the source

  • The level's own water (not under its children, not behind its barriers): 520 km² on the grid, 518 km² deeper than min_depth in the source.
  • Source water in the level's dry cells: 3.7 km² (0.7 %), 2.6 million m³. In its wet cells 1.2 % of the source cells are land or shallower.
  • Inside a wet cell the source depths spread by 0.3 m (p90 − p10, median over the cells; 1.2 m in the 10 % most varied). The block mean lies at the 50th percentile of its cell's source depths (10–90 % of the cells: 42th–56th).
  • 3 basins of at least 4 cells with no connection found in the source (deeper than min_depth): 4637 cells at 8.07°E 55.94°N; 6 cells at 8.12°E 56.00°N; 4 cells at 8.20°E 55.86°N.

Thalwegs

Source water deeper than 4 m (then down to 0.5 m and 0.5 m for basins still cut off, also through other basins) that crosses the level's dry cells and joins two basins (gap) or two parts of one basin much further apart around it (shortcut). Through each, the path whose shallowest point is deepest; each dry cell on it gets the p50 of the source depths along the path in that cell, not its deepest point, and at least 2.5 m. All of them are applied in the level's bathymetry (config: thalwegs: apply in dkm.yaml).

No gaps or shortcuts: the grid keeps every connection of the source deeper than min_depth.
